# DUZZA GROUP — Project context

## Project objective
Build one fast, mobile-first, accessible, search-friendly corporate website for **DUZZA GROUP**, the public-facing parent brand. The website must establish the parent company, give its two active business arms distinct and credible experiences, and convert relevant visitors into qualified enquiries. The structure must support additional divisions later without rebuilding the site.

## Naming and legal identity
- Public-facing parent brand: **DUZZA GROUP** (double Z).
- Legal company name stated in the client brief: **DUZZA LIMITED**.
- The final legal/footer wording and any company registration display must be confirmed against the client's current CAC records before launch.
- Do not interchange DUZZA GROUP and DUZZA LIMITED as if they were the same type of name. Use DUZZA GROUP for brand-facing headings unless the context is explicitly legal.

## Active business arms

### Duzza Projects (working name)
The client brief describes general contracting and general merchants, with work areas including:
- Engineering and construction projects.
- Oil and gas projects.
- Marine project support.
- Marine / hydro-spatial / hydrographic survey support.
- Hire of hydrographic navigation and positioning equipment.
- General supply, procurement and merchandise trading.

Geographic wording: the client clarified that engineering currently has Nigeria reach. The strategy may make the website accessible to and relevant for international enquiries where appropriate, but must not claim international project delivery, operational coverage or offices unless verified. Do not imply equipment is currently available for hire until an up-to-date inventory is supplied.

### Duzza Branding
Services and work described in the brief:
- Executive branding for companies, agencies and executives.
- Corporate and digital branding.
- Creative support, media and graphic content.
- UGC-style video, static promotional images, graphic content and campaign assets.
- Sports-brand promotional content.
- Merchandising and branded/customised promotional items.

The client clarified that this arm is not confined to Nigeria, has customers outside Nigeria, and its target audience is mostly outside Nigeria. Write international-facing copy and avoid framing the division as Nigeria-only. The brief identifies the existing Instagram channel as `@duzza_branding`; link to it only after verifying the live account and approved assets.

## Future divisions
Treat the following as **Coming soon** or **In development**, not as currently trading services:
- Fashion / clothing: boutique, English and Ankara wear, shoes, bags, branded/customised clothing.
- Bakery, pastries, confectionery, restaurant/eatery and fast food.

Additional future businesses are mentioned in the brief but not fully listed. Do not invent them.

## Primary audiences
- Corporate, oil and gas, marine and procurement teams seeking contracting, survey support, equipment hire or supply.
- Sports brands, agencies, executives and companies seeking branding and creative services, including international prospects.
- Future fashion and food customers who may want launch updates.
- Partners and other general business contacts.

## Primary conversion goals
1. Project / contracting / procurement enquiries.
2. Equipment hire enquiries, once the equipment catalogue is supplied.
3. Branding project briefs.
4. General and partnership enquiries.
5. Optional, consent-based interest capture for future divisions, only when a real inbox and owner are configured.

## Brand and experience direction
- Contemporary, confident, premium, clear and trustworthy.
- Clean editorial layout with generous whitespace and strong typography.
- Technical precision for Projects; expressive, image-led presentation for Branding.
- Mobile-first and resilient on variable network connections.
- Restrained motion; never let animation interfere with reading, navigation or performance.
- Avoid dated engineering templates, visual clutter, heavy 3D, excessive glass effects and decorative animation without purpose.

## Logo decision
The client prefers the monogram from one logo iteration and the spelled-out wordmark from another. The design task is to combine the approved source artwork into one coherent lockup, matching optical weight, alignment, spacing and proportions. Do not redraw, approximate or fabricate the selected logo. The source artwork is not included in this package.
- Desktop header: combined monogram + DUZZA GROUP wordmark.
- Mobile: use the combined lockup if it remains legible at the available width; otherwise use the monogram with an accessible text name for assistive technology.
- Favicon/social avatar: monogram.
- Await final approved source files and brand-owner approval before treating the lockup as final.

## Factual integrity rules
Never fabricate or imply:
- clients, project names, case studies, testimonials or client logos;
- countries served, offices, geographic coverage or project scale;
- years of experience, number of projects, team size, performance metrics or results;
- equipment inventory, specifications, availability or certifications;
- CAC registration number, registered address, licences or memberships;
- contact details, response-time promises, launch dates or waitlist operations.

Use explicit content placeholders such as `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: ...]`, `[CLIENT TO VERIFY: ...]` and `[APPROVAL REQUIRED]`. Conceptual imagery must be labelled internally and must never be presented as real DUZZA project evidence.
