# DUZZA GROUP — Content specification

## Global content rules
- Use DUZZA GROUP as the public parent brand; use DUZZA LIMITED only in verified legal contexts.
- Do not invent facts, clients, outcomes, certifications, locations, equipment or metrics.
- Mark all missing content with `[CLIENT TO SUPPLY: ...]` or `[CLIENT TO VERIFY: ...]` during development.
- Do not expose internal placeholders on the live site. Missing launch-critical content must block publishing or be omitted cleanly.
- Avoid vague claims such as “world-class”, “leading”, “number one” or “trusted by the best” unless evidence is supplied.

## Home
Required content:
- One clear parent-level positioning line.
- Short supporting paragraph explaining that DUZZA GROUP operates distinct business divisions.
- Two primary CTAs: Projects enquiry and Branding project.
- Active division summaries.
- A concise group story/why-us section.
- Verified work or capability highlights.
- Optional future venture teasers.

Content still needed:
- Approved parent positioning statement.
- Approved company story, mission, vision and values.
- Verified images and project/creative examples.
- Final contact details.

## About
Required content:
- Group overview and origin story.
- Vision and mission (client-approved wording).
- Values expressed as observable behaviours, not empty adjectives.
- Leadership names, titles, biographies and approved photographs if the client wants profiles.
- Group/division structure.
- Legal details only after verification: exact legal entity wording, RC number and registered office.

The development brief mentions a possible leadership name; do not publish a name or profile until the client confirms the person, title and biography.

## Duzza Projects
Explain the division's stated capabilities in plain language:
- General contracting and general merchants.
- Engineering and construction projects.
- Oil and gas projects.
- Marine project support.
- Hydro-spatial / hydrographic survey support.
- Hire of hydrographic navigation and positioning equipment.
- General supply, procurement and merchandise trading.

For each capability, request:
- Exact service description and scope boundaries.
- Industries and client types served.
- Delivery geography that can be substantiated.
- Relevant equipment, personnel and qualifications where applicable.
- Process, documentation and safety/compliance statements approved for publication.
- Approved images and case evidence.

Do not publish detailed technical claims, equipment specs or certifications from inference.

## Duzza Branding
Positioning must address an international audience. Services to represent:
1. Executive Branding — personal/leadership brand identity, profile imagery, bios, LinkedIn/social presence, speaker and press kits (only those actually offered).
2. Corporate and Digital Branding — identity refresh, brand guidelines, social media design systems, website/campaign visuals and digital ad creatives (confirm final service scope).
3. Sports and Creative Media — UGC-style video, static promotional images, graphic content and campaign assets.
4. Merchandising — branded/customised apparel and promotional items, concept-to-production support (clarify what production responsibilities DUZZA directly undertakes).

Portfolio/case study fields:
- Client name or anonymised sector label.
- Country/market, only if approved and accurate.
- Challenge or brief.
- DUZZA's exact contribution and deliverables.
- Media gallery (original assets with rights cleared).
- Outcomes/impact, only when measured or approved.
- Testimonial, only with written permission.

Instagram: use curated original assets supplied by the client. Do not depend on an API/live feed for the initial build. Link to the verified `@duzza_branding` account.

## Equipment catalogue
Publish only after receiving:
- Equipment name and category.
- Model/specification and intended use.
- Current availability status and who maintains it.
- Approved images and downloadable datasheets.
- Hire geography and enquiry contact.

No pricing or availability claims unless supplied and maintained.

## Future divisions
Fashion and Food/Hospitality pages must visibly state `Coming soon` or `In development`. Do not list products, menus, prices, delivery promises, launch dates or ordering functionality until those businesses are operational and details are approved.
